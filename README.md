# Digital Al-Jazari for AI & Research Innovation

**Where AI Meets Research Innovation | حيث يلتقي الذكاء بالابتكار**

Digital Al-Jazari is a Jordanian nonprofit organization established in 2015. We connect artificial intelligence, applied research, water sustainability, climate resilience, education, cultural heritage, and humanitarian knowledge to develop locally grounded ideas with wider relevance.

Inspired by the genius of Al-Jazari, we believe that heritage is not merely a memory—it is a source of knowledge, creativity, and future innovation.

## Featured Initiatives

### Reviving Umm Al-Jimal’s Water Wisdom

A public research and innovation initiative documenting the ancient rainwater-harvesting system of Umm Al-Jimal and exploring its contemporary relevance to water security, climate resilience, ecosystem restoration, and peacebuilding in drylands.

[Explore the Umm Al-Jimal Water Wisdom repository](https://github.com/DigitalAljazari/Reviving-Umm-Al-Jimal-Water-Wisdom)

### Mustanef — Transformative AI Readiness for LMICs

A strategic initiative strengthening preparedness for transformative artificial intelligence in low- and middle-income countries through research, policy, strategic foresight, public dialogue, partnerships, and ANAwAI.

[Explore the Mustanef repository](https://github.com/DigitalAljazari/Mustanef-Transformative-AI-Readiness-for-LMICs)

## Emerging Strategic Direction

Digital Al-Jazari is developing the **Drylands Blue–Green Resilience Bond** framework, linking inland water security with terrestrial ecosystem recovery, measurable resilience outcomes, safeguards, and pathways for climate-finance partnerships.

This is a proposed research and policy framework—not an issued or authorized financial instrument.
